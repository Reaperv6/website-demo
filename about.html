About

This is the about page of my personal studio website. Here you can find information about me and my work.

My Life Behind the Drums

Music has been a part of my life for as long as I can remember. I started playing drums when I was around three years old, and over the years, drumming has grown from something I enjoyed into one of my biggest passions. As I got older, I started exploring different genres of music, eventually finding my way into metal and deathcore. I became drawn to the intensity, technicality, heavy breakdowns, and incredible drumming that make these genres unique. Bands and drummers in the metal scene inspired me to push my own abilities, experiment with different techniques, and challenge myself to play more difficult music. Today, drumming is more than just a hobby to me. It’s a way to express myself, connect with the music I love, and continue improving as a musician. This website is where I share that passion through drum covers, showcasing the songs, bands, and music that have influenced my journey.
